# Dashboard — `dashboard/`

System section of UGC OS. This file loads when Claude works with files under `src/apps/dashboard/`; the app-wide rules stay in the root [CLAUDE.md](../../../CLAUDE.md).

## Job

Default landing page, staged as a **desktop in space**: a wallpaper layer (`components/DesktopWallpaper` — three colour blooms, two drifting star layers and a meteor, all dark-mode only except the blooms; classes in `index.css` under `.desktop-*`; shared with Edit, which is why it lives in `components/`, and it takes **no props** — a `tint` prop for a second bloom palette lasted one commit and came out when both pages wanted the same corner. The top-left bloom is **neutral light**, not an accent, so neither page leads on one app's colour: white in dark mode, a soft shadow in light, where a white bloom on a white canvas would be invisible. B-Roll violet and the crab's gold hold the other two corners), the value widgets laid across it, and the crew orbiting as a **solar system** on the right. **Widgets** (`Widget.tsx` — `WIDGET_SHELL` is the one glass material: translucent fill + backdrop-blur/saturate + a 1px inner top highlight, white fill in light mode; `.widget-rise` staggers them in on open): time saved (per-kind minutes + a task-switching constant, both in `utils/usage.ts`) with a 14-day sparkline (`dailyMinutesSaved`), money saved vs the higher of official-API and creator-platform pricing with a paid-vs-elsewhere bar — both heroes carry a green rolling-7-day "+N this week" delta — a **streak ring** (`StreakRing`, the signature: the arc is the current streak against the member's own best, falling back to a 7-day target before there is a record, with a lit marker riding its head), the 26-week activity heatmap, and — stacked in the row's last three columns — the **Announcements** tile (`AnnouncementsTile`, siren glyph + red unread dot, opens the log panel; see the Announcements bullet in the root file) above Academy. Those two are **stacked, not side by side**: the slot is ~177px, which halves into squares too narrow for either title, and giving them a column each drops Activity below nine columns, where the heatmap grows an internal scrollbar. Both are compact icon-left cards with truncating titles, since each gets half the row's height. **The wall is two rows and must stay two rows** — the whole desktop has to sit inside one window alongside the dock, and a member who has to scroll the landing page to see the heatmap reads it as a page, not a desktop. That budget is what retired the separate **Best streak** and **Active days** tiles: three streak-shaped numbers in a row, now sub-lines on the widgets that own them (the record under the ring it is measured against, the day count in the heatmap's caption). **`SolarSystem.tsx`** is the launcher and the one big swing: eight apps as eight planets on faint orbit rings around a gold sun, each planet the app's dock glyph on a sphere mixed from its own accent. Orbits run 5–15 minutes (halving that read as drift, not depth), the arm and the planet counter-rotate on the same period so glyphs stay upright, and hovering anywhere freezes the system so picking one is never a moving-target problem. **Every orbit arm is `pointer-events-none`** and only the planet takes events back: the arms are eight full-size boxes stacked on each other, so without that the topmost one (Playground, the outermost planet) swallows every hover and click meant for the seven underneath — the whole orrery reads as broken except its last planet. Eight labels in orbit would collide, so one caption slot under it names whatever the cursor is on, crab and all. Geometry is in px, so **below `xl` it's hidden** and `DesktopIcons` renders the plain crew grid instead. Until a kie key is saved, a notification-style connect banner (`ConnectKeyCard` → `ApiKeyGuide`) sits above the widgets. Everything derives from the `usageDays` ledger (see Banks) — the app never writes data. The menu bar shows a flame "N day streak" chip (opens Dashboard) whenever the streak is live.
