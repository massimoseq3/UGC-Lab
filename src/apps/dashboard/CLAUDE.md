# Dashboard — `dashboard/`

System section of UGC OS. This file loads when Claude works with files under `src/apps/dashboard/`; the app-wide rules stay in the root [CLAUDE.md](../../../CLAUDE.md).

## Job

Default landing page: the value widgets laid across **`AppBackground`, the same gradient every other page shows** — no background of its own. It got there in two steps, both Massimo's call (September 2026): first the wallpaper's three white blooms and vignette went, then the starfield itself (`components/DesktopWallpaper`, shared with Edit, deleted with its `.desktop-stars-*` classes). What's left is that this page and Edit are the same room as the rest of the workspace. **Don't reintroduce a background layer here** — and if one ever is, nothing in it may move: see the idle-page rule in [docs/performance.md](../../../docs/performance.md), which is also why the orrery is gone. **Widgets** (`Widget.tsx` — `WIDGET_SHELL` is the one glass material: translucent fill + backdrop-blur/saturate + a 1px inner top highlight, white fill in light mode; `.widget-rise` staggers them in on open).

**The wall is THREE COLUMNS, and the right-hand one is a single tile two rows tall** (September 2026, Massimo's call). **Time saved** over **Money saved** down the left, **Activity** over **Academy** in the middle, and **What's New** filling the right. Above it, the wall was six identical `lg:col-span-4` squares in a 3x2 grid (August 2026), everything in them centred — that shape is still what every tile but What's New is drawn for, and the centring rule below still governs all of them. What changed is that one tile earned a column: a list of videos wants height, not width, and there is no height in a 233px square. **The Streak ring went with the rearrangement** — `StreakRing.tsx` is deleted; the menu bar's flame chip still carries the number and `computeUsageMetrics` still derives it, so nothing about the streak itself was removed.

**Grid mechanics, because DOM order is not reading order here.** Items are placed in the order Time saved → Activity → What's New → Money saved → Academy, so auto-flow drops the last two into row two beside the tall column; `slot(n)` re-numbers the load stagger back into reading order. `auto-rows-fr` is **`lg:` only** — equal rows are what make the desktop read as a wall, and below that breakpoint they would squeeze What's New into a figure tile's height.

The tiles: the 13-week **activity heatmap** (`ActivityHeatmap`); **time saved** (per-kind minutes + a task-switching constant, both in `utils/usage.ts`) with a 14-day sparkline (`dailyMinutesSaved`); **money saved** vs the higher of official-API and creator-platform pricing, with a paid-vs-elsewhere bar; **What's New** (`WhatsNewTile`, below); and the **Academy** link.

**A figure tile stacks from its label DOWN, and only its chart takes `mt-auto`.** Bottom-aligning the whole block landed the hero at a different height in each tile, because Money saved's floor art (a bar plus two captions) is taller than a sparkline and pushed its block further up — so the two heroes, captions and deltas sat a good 10px out of line with each other. Text from the top, chart on the floor, and the pair line up by construction. The order inside is **figure → caption → delta pill**: `WidgetDelta` is its own line under the caption rather than a chip on the hero's baseline, which only worked while a tile was wide enough for both (in a four-column tile it wrapped under a 56px hero and read as that number's caption, displacing the real one). **`WidgetLabel` carries no trailing note** — Money saved's credit count was the only one, and `ml-auto` on a note is exactly what a centred label can't have.

**The wall is two rows and must stay two rows** — the whole desktop has to sit inside one window alongside the dock, and a member who has to scroll the landing page to see the heatmap reads it as a page, not a desktop.

**Below `lg` it is a two-across bento** (August 2026): every widget was `col-span-12` there, so a phone got six full-width slabs stacked one under another and the desktop became a page you scroll. Two rows of two — **What's New is the one exception and keeps the full width**, because its rows are pictures, and it takes `order-last` so the phone still ends on it rather than splitting the four figures around it. **Nothing in a half-width tile is allowed to wrap, and every second line is the first thing to go** — the phone wall has to fit one screen alongside the dock, and each extra line costs it ~26px. What that means per widget: `WidgetFigure`'s hero drops to 34px below `sm` (48px is wider than the box), **`WidgetDelta` hides entirely**, Activity's generations/active-days tally hides, `SpendBar`'s two captions go to 10px on one `whitespace-nowrap` line, and the Academy card's out-of-flow ↗ goes, since out of flow it reserved no space and simply landed on the last letter of the title. Money saved's sub-line is **one string plus a `hidden sm:inline` tail** rather than two strings: a phone reads "vs official APIs", a desktop gets the rest. Time saved's is a bare `N workdays` at every width — "≈ 7.6 workdays of production and tool-hopping" was a sentence explaining a number that needs no explaining, and the ≈ hedged a figure the hero above it states exactly.

**`ActivityHeatmap` is 13 columns, has no month labels, and SIZES ITSELF to whatever tile it is dropped in.** Every column takes `flex-1 min-w-0 max-w-[18px]` and every cell `aspect-square w-full`, so the blocks come out as big as the row allows and the picture ends where the tile does. Three things were fixed at once here in August 2026 (all Massimo's calls). It ran **26** columns, half a year, which at the tile's own width drew cells too small to read and spent most of the picture on months a member has stopped thinking about — a quarter is the recent run, which is what this widget is looked at for. It drew at a **fixed 11px** per cell above `sm`, making it a fixed-size object in a fluid box: a dead margin down its right wherever the tile was wider (reported as "a massive gap" the moment the legend that had been filling it came out) and an `overflow-x-auto` scrollbar wherever it was narrower — both the same bug, and filling has no width to be right or wrong about. The `max-w` is the one limit, because 13 square cells with no ceiling reach ~24px in a full-width tile and the grid's HEIGHT follows its cell size, which would grow the whole second row with the window; capped, a wide tile centres the grid instead — even margins, which read as placement where a one-sided margin reads as a gap. And the **month labels** went with the 26 columns: at thirteen the picture is "the recent run", not a calendar to find a date in. **There is no legend either.** A `HeatmapLegend` ("Less ▪▪▪▪ More") sat beside the grid at `xl` and is deleted: darker means more is the one thing about this picture nobody has to be told. All of it is CSS-only, so there is no media query in JS.

**What's New is ONE feed of two sources: Massimo's YouTube channel and the operator's announcements** (`WhatsNewTile`, September 2026, Massimo's call). It was the Announcements log (August 2026) and took the videos in alongside them because the channel is where the training actually lands and a member on the Dashboard every morning is the audience for it. They share one tile because the wall is a fixed set of tiles, and they share one **door** — an announcement row opens the existing `AnnouncementsPanel`, and it is the only thing that does, so the list may never be built in a way that can leave zero announcement rows showing while announcements exist. Rows merge **pinned first, then newest** (`byRecency`, the announcement store's own order extended over the videos), a video row is an `<a target="_blank">` straight to YouTube, and the unread dot is **out of flow** at the tile's corner for the same reason `WidgetLabel` has no note — it counts unread announcements AND unseen videos, because a member needs one light, not two.

**It shows FIVE rows with 124px thumbnails, and that is the fourth shape it wore.** Each earlier one is a thing not to rebuild: three truncated rows in a single-height tile read as a list you scan past; a full-width 16:9 hero with a **See All** under it spent a whole column on one video (the hero had to be letterboxed to 88px to fit a 233px tile at all, which cut the faces off the thumbnails); and the entire eleven-video log at 53px a thumbnail fit the tall column, but at that size the picture is a swatch and the row is really just its title. **The thumbnail is what makes someone click, so it gets the space** — and the date came off with the small rows, since a video from July is not more or less worth watching for saying so and the column it took came off the title. There is no See All: the rest of the channel is one click away on YouTube itself.

Three mechanics hold that up. The row's height is the **block's**, and the text column centres inside it, which is what lets `NewBadge` take a line above the title on some rows without making those rows taller. The list takes `justify-between` so five rows spread down whatever height the wall hands the tile rather than bunching under the label. And the list must never DRIVE that height: a full log in flow made the tile 510px and the wall 30px taller than the rest of the page budgets for. An announcement row wears the same block with a `Megaphone` in it, so the two sources keep one rhythm.

**The video list is hand-maintained, in `utils/channelVideos.ts`** — Massimo posts a video, tells Claude, and the array grows by one line at the top. Not the YouTube Data API (a key, a quota and a billing account to keep alive) and not the channel's RSS feed (CORS-blocked from a browser, so it would need a route in `api/`, a host on the fetch-media allowlist and a cache) — for something that changes once a fortnight and that he is present for anyway. Thumbnails come off `i.ytimg.com` through `youtubeThumb(id, 'mq')`, so there is nothing to store and nothing to expire; **`mq` not `hq`** matters, because `hqdefault` is a 4:3 frame with a 16:9 video letterboxed inside it. Which videos have been seen lives in `stores/videoLogStore` — **browser-local, deliberately**, and its first run seeds everything but the newest as seen so a new member gets one badge rather than eleven.

**A figure tile with no floor art yet CENTRES its number** (September 2026, Massimo's call): both charts bail before there is data — `Sparkline` on a flat zero peak, `SpendBar` on nothing spent elsewhere — which left "0 min" pinned under its label with the bottom half of the tile visibly empty, reading as a widget missing a piece rather than one waiting for its first generation. `hasSpark` / `hasSpend` mirror each chart's own bail condition so the tile and its floor can't disagree. The "NOT `mt-auto`" rule above still holds for the populated state, and the pair still line up with each other here — neither has a chart to be pushed up by, which is the exact condition that rule is about. **Academy keeps the centred disc-over-title card** — it is the wall's one pure link and the one tile with nothing of the member's own in it, so it takes no `WidgetLabel` header. **Its disc and title step up from `sm`** (52px / 18px, September 2026, Massimo's call): it carries two short words where the other five carry a figure, so at the five's supporting sizes it read as the quietest thing on a wall of equal squares. The phone keeps the smaller pair — the tile is half a screen wide there and "AI UGC Academy" is one line by a hair. The two wore one shared shape (`SHORTCUT_TILE` in `Widget.tsx`) for exactly as long as they were the same card; when Announcements became a log the constant went back inline, since a shared abstraction with one consumer is worse than a className.

**Centring is now the whole wall's rule, not a phone treatment** — see the six-identical-tiles note above. Two things stay put: **`SpendBar`'s two figures keep `justify-between` at every width**, because they label the two ENDS of the bar above them and centring them detaches each number from what it measures; and **`WidgetLabel`'s icon is monochrome and 13px**, not 15px in the dashboard green, since an eyebrow's whole job is to be quiet and a 15px accent glyph against 11px caps was both the loudest thing on the row and visibly taller than the letters it labels.

**The greeting is ONE face on one line** — `DISPLAY_FONT`, **italic, `font-normal`, `tracking-tighter`** (August 2026, Massimo's call). It was two for a stint, Geist for the salutation and Instrument Serif for the name alone on the reasoning that the name is the one word on the page about this member; the masthead reads better as a single mark, and it went back. It stays at `font-normal`: Instrument Serif ships a single weight, so `font-bold` here only asks the browser to synthesize one, which thickens the strokes without the face ever drawing a real bold — tried for an hour and reverted. **Centred at every width, with `AppLogo` above it** (September 2026, Massimo's call) — it was centred on a phone and left-aligned from `sm`, like the wall under it. The brand mark is what changed the alignment: a logo pinned to the left edge over a wall of centred tiles reads as a page header, where the point of it here is the masthead of the desktop underneath. It's the one page in the app that isn't a working surface, so it's the one that can be centred. It's Title Case — "Good Morning", not "Good morning": this is the page's masthead, not a sentence, and it reads as one beside the member's own name in the display face. The line under it is **the date alone** for a member with any activity; "Here's what UGC OS has saved you so far" said nothing the four widgets underneath don't say themselves. The empty-state half stays, since a member with no numbers yet needs telling where they'd come from. It renders 30px on a phone rather than 36, so "Good afternoon, <name>" can't gain a second line and push the bento's last row under the fold. **`WidgetLabel` takes the app's field-label treatment**, not one of its own: 11px, `font-medium`, `text-ink-300` — the same eyebrow `SectionLabel` gives Characters' GENDER / AGE RANGE and every Bank form. It was semibold and a step dimmer, which is one house style too many for a word doing the same job in both places. **Tracking is the one thing it does differently**: `0.07em` against `SectionLabel`'s `widest` (Massimo's call, August 2026). A field label sits over a form and has a column to be spaced across; these sit at the top of a half-width bento tile beside a 13px glyph, where `0.1em` pushed TIME SAVED / ACTIVITY out to the tile's edges and read as stretched rather than as quiet. That budget is what retired the separate **Best streak** and **Active days** tiles: three streak-shaped numbers in a row, now sub-lines on the widgets that own them (the record under the ring it is measured against, the day count in the heatmap's caption). **The crew used to orbit the wall as a solar system** — nine planets on nine rotating arms around a gold sun, hidden below `xl`, one `ORBITS` entry zipped against each `utils/team.ts` member. It is deleted (September 2026, Massimo's call). It was the page's one big swing and it was also permanent motion on the app's default landing page, under a wall of `backdrop-filter` widgets — the one shape [docs/performance.md](../../../docs/performance.md) tells you not to build; measured at ~4 points of one CPU core for as long as the Dashboard was on screen. The dock is the launcher, and it always was below `xl`. Until a kie key is saved, a notification-style connect banner (`ConnectKeyCard` → `ApiKeyGuide`) sits above the widgets. Everything derives from the `usageDays` ledger (see Banks) — the app never writes data. The menu bar shows a flame "N day streak" chip (opens Dashboard) whenever the streak is live.
