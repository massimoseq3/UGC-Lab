# Dashboard — `dashboard/`

System section of UGC OS. This file loads when Claude works with files under `src/apps/dashboard/`; the app-wide rules stay in the root [CLAUDE.md](../../../CLAUDE.md).

## Job

Default landing page, staged as a **desktop in space**: a wallpaper layer (`components/DesktopWallpaper` — three colour blooms, two drifting star layers and a meteor, all dark-mode only except the blooms; classes in `index.css` under `.desktop-*`; shared with Edit, which is why it lives in `components/`, and it takes **no props** — a `tint` prop for a second bloom palette lasted one commit and came out when both pages wanted the same corner. **All three blooms are neutral light**, not accents, so neither page leads on one app's colour: white in dark mode, a soft shadow in light, where a white bloom on a white canvas would be invisible. Each has now been an accent and lost it — the top left Dashboard emerald, the top right B-Roll violet, the bottom the crab's gold (August 2026) — because an accent in the sky competes with the nine accents of the planets orbiting in front of it, which is the one thing on this page that has to read as colour, and the gold was the widest of them, washing the whole lower half warm. The sun in `SolarSystem` keeps its gold: that one IS the picture), the value widgets laid across it, and the crew orbiting as a **solar system** on the right. **Widgets** (`Widget.tsx` — `WIDGET_SHELL` is the one glass material: translucent fill + backdrop-blur/saturate + a 1px inner top highlight, white fill in light mode; `.widget-rise` staggers them in on open): time saved (per-kind minutes + a task-switching constant, both in `utils/usage.ts`) with a 14-day sparkline (`dailyMinutesSaved`), money saved vs the higher of official-API and creator-platform pricing with a paid-vs-elsewhere bar — both heroes carry a green rolling-7-day "+N this week" delta — a **streak ring** (`StreakRing`, the signature: the arc is the current streak against the member's own best, falling back to a 7-day target before there is a record, with a lit marker riding its head), the 26-week activity heatmap, and — ending the row, a column each — the **Announcements** tile (`AnnouncementsTile`, siren glyph + red unread dot, opens the log panel; see the Announcements bullet in the root file) beside Academy. Those two were **stacked** in one ~177px slot until August 2026 (Massimo's call), because a column each left ~113px per card and Activity couldn't fund the width: its 26-week grid needs ~361px and below nine columns it grew an internal scrollbar. **Dropping the heatmap's legend paid for both** — Activity holds the grid at seven columns with ~40px to spare, and each card takes ~145px, which is a tile you can title. **The wall is two rows and must stay two rows** — the whole desktop has to sit inside one window alongside the dock, and a member who has to scroll the landing page to see the heatmap reads it as a page, not a desktop. **Below `lg` it is a two-across bento instead** (August 2026): every widget was `col-span-12` there, so a phone got six full-width slabs stacked one under another and the desktop became a page you scroll. Three rows of two, in DOM order — the two figures, then Streak and Activity, then the Announcements/Academy pair, each in a square of its own. The whole desktop fits one phone screen again. **Nothing in a half-width tile is allowed to wrap**, and what that costs is spelled out per widget: `WidgetFigure`'s hero drops to 34px below `sm` (48px is wider than the box), `WidgetLabel`'s right-hand note hides, `SpendBar`'s two captions go to 10px on one `whitespace-nowrap` line, Activity's generations/active-days note hides below `sm`, and the Academy card's out-of-flow ↗ goes, since out of flow it reserved no space and simply landed on the last letter of the title. **Activity carries no empty-state caption at any width** — "Every generation lights up a day" sat in that slot on the reasoning that a blank 26-week grid reads as broken rather than waiting, and came out in August 2026 (Massimo's call): the label already says Activity and the empty grid already says there hasn't been any. The two long sub-lines are **one string plus a `hidden sm:inline` tail** rather than two strings: a phone reads "≈ 7.1 workdays" and "vs official APIs", a desktop gets the rest of the sentence. Activity earns its half tile in `ActivityHeatmap` — below `sm` only the last `PHONE_WEEKS` columns render (the rest take `max-sm:hidden`), which is a quarter of the history at a size you can read rather than half a year squeezed into 128px. Those columns then **FILL the tile** rather than drawing at a fixed size: each takes `flex-1 min-w-0` and each cell `aspect-square w-full`, so the blocks come out as big as the row allows (~10px) instead of 8px squares against a margin of empty tile. From `sm` it's `sm:flex-none` + the fixed `11px` grid the 26-week desktop row is sized around, and only that half keeps the `overflow-x-auto`. **There is no legend.** A `HeatmapLegend` ("Less ▪▪▪▪ More") sat beside the grid at `xl` and is deleted (August 2026, Massimo's call): darker means more is the one thing about this picture nobody has to be told, and the ~110px it held is exactly what the two shortcut cards needed to take a column each. All of it is CSS-only, so there is no media query in JS and the desktop wall is untouched by construction.

**The Announcements and Academy cards are ONE card twice** — same disc, title and sub-line, one a button and one a link — so the shape lives in `Widget.tsx` as `SHORTCUT_TILE` (+ `_DISC` / `_GLYPH` / `_TEXT` / `_TITLE` / `_SUB`) rather than being written out in two files that drift. It has **two layouts, chosen by the tile's proportions and not by screen size**: a centred column (icon over title over sub-line) below `sm` AND again from `lg`, where the card is roughly as tall as it is wide — the phone's half-bento square, and the desktop's own column at the end of the wall's second row — and an icon-left row in between, where the pair share one full-width bento row and each card is wide and short. **Truncation belongs to the row layout only** (`sm:truncate`, put back by `lg:whitespace-normal`): there the card is half a row tall and a wrapped second title line pushes the sub-line clean out of the box, while both centred layouts have the height for two lines and none of the width to lose to an ellipsis.

**Everything is CENTRED below `sm`, and nothing is above it** (August 2026, Massimo's call): on a phone the greeting, the date and every stat widget's contents — label, hero figure, sub-line, heatmap — sit on the tile's centre line, because a half-width bento tile reads better that way; from `sm` the wall is the desktop wall and every one of those is left-aligned exactly as it was. It's all `max-sm:` for that reason, so the desktop is untouched by construction. `max-sm:items-center` on a `Widget` makes every child shrink to its content, which is why anything that must span the tile (the figure block, the heatmap row, the spend bar) also says `w-full`. **`SpendBar`'s two figures keep `justify-between` at every width** — they label the two ENDS of the bar above them, and centring them detaches each number from what it measures. **`WidgetLabel`'s icon is monochrome and 13px**, not 15px in the dashboard green: an eyebrow's whole job is to be quiet, and a 15px accent glyph against 11px caps was both the loudest thing on the row and visibly taller than the letters it labels.

**The greeting is ONE face on one line** — `DISPLAY_FONT`, **italic, `font-normal`, `tracking-tighter`** (August 2026, Massimo's call). It was two for a stint, Geist for the salutation and Instrument Serif for the name alone on the reasoning that the name is the one word on the page about this member; the masthead reads better as a single mark, and it went back. It stays at `font-normal`: Instrument Serif ships a single weight, so `font-bold` here only asks the browser to synthesize one, which thickens the strokes without the face ever drawing a real bold — tried for an hour and reverted. Centred on a phone, left-aligned from `sm`, like the wall under it. It's Title Case — "Good Morning", not "Good morning": this is the page's masthead, not a sentence, and it reads as one beside the member's own name in the display face. The line under it is **the date alone** for a member with any activity; "Here's what UGC OS has saved you so far" said nothing the four widgets underneath don't say themselves. The empty-state half stays, since a member with no numbers yet needs telling where they'd come from. It renders 30px on a phone rather than 36, so "Good afternoon, <name>" can't gain a second line and push the bento's last row under the fold. **`WidgetLabel` takes the app's field-label treatment**, not one of its own: 11px, `font-medium`, `text-ink-300` — the same eyebrow `SectionLabel` gives Characters' GENDER / AGE RANGE and every Bank form. It was semibold and a step dimmer, which is one house style too many for a word doing the same job in both places. **Tracking is the one thing it does differently**: `0.07em` against `SectionLabel`'s `widest` (Massimo's call, August 2026). A field label sits over a form and has a column to be spaced across; these sit at the top of a half-width bento tile beside a 13px glyph, where `0.1em` pushed TIME SAVED / ACTIVITY out to the tile's edges and read as stretched rather than as quiet. That budget is what retired the separate **Best streak** and **Active days** tiles: three streak-shaped numbers in a row, now sub-lines on the widgets that own them (the record under the ring it is measured against, the day count in the heatmap's caption). **`SolarSystem.tsx`** is the launcher and the one big swing: eight apps as eight planets on faint orbit rings around a gold sun, each planet the app's dock glyph on a sphere mixed from its own accent. Orbits run 5–15 minutes (halving that read as drift, not depth), the arm and the planet counter-rotate on the same period so glyphs stay upright, and hovering anywhere freezes the system so picking one is never a moving-target problem. **Every orbit arm is `pointer-events-none`** and only the planet takes events back: the arms are eight full-size boxes stacked on each other, so without that the topmost one (Playground, the outermost planet) swallows every hover and click meant for the seven underneath — the whole orrery reads as broken except its last planet. **A planet's hover halo is a radial gradient BEHIND the sphere**, and both halves of that are the fix for one bug: as a later sibling with `blur()` on it, the halo painted a flat 70%-opacity disc of accent over the planet's own face, so hovering turned a shaded ball into a fuzzy blob and took its rim light with it — and the button `scale`s on that same hover, which re-rasters a filtered layer every frame of the transition (the thing the blob-drift note in `index.css` says not to do). A gradient is painted once and composited, the same idiom `DesktopIcons`' tile glow already used. Eight labels in orbit would collide, so one caption slot under it names whatever the cursor is on, crab and all. Geometry is in px, so **below `xl` it's hidden and NOTHING takes its place** (August 2026, Massimo's call). A `DesktopIcons` grid of the nine crabs stood in there for a stint and is deleted: at that width it sat directly above the dock — the same nine apps, in the same order, wearing the same glyphs — so a phone rendered the launcher twice and the second copy was the one you had to scroll to. The orrery is a desktop flourish; on a small screen the dock is how you open an app. Until a kie key is saved, a notification-style connect banner (`ConnectKeyCard` → `ApiKeyGuide`) sits above the widgets. Everything derives from the `usageDays` ledger (see Banks) — the app never writes data. The menu bar shows a flame "N day streak" chip (opens Dashboard) whenever the streak is live.
