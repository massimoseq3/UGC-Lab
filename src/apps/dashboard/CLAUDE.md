# Dashboard — `dashboard/`

System section of UGC OS. This file loads when Claude works with files under `src/apps/dashboard/`; the app-wide rules stay in the root [CLAUDE.md](../../../CLAUDE.md).

## Job

Default landing page: the value widgets laid across **`AppBackground`, the same gradient every other page shows** — no background of its own. It got there in two steps, both Massimo's call (September 2026): first the wallpaper's three white blooms and vignette went, then the starfield itself (`components/DesktopWallpaper`, shared with Edit, deleted with its `.desktop-stars-*` classes). What's left is that this page and Edit are the same room as the rest of the workspace. **Don't reintroduce a background layer here** — and if one ever is, nothing in it may move: see the idle-page rule in [docs/performance.md](../../../docs/performance.md), which is also why the orrery is gone. **Widgets** (`Widget.tsx` — `WIDGET_SHELL` is the one glass material: translucent fill + backdrop-blur/saturate + a 1px inner top highlight, white fill in light mode; `.widget-rise` staggers them in on open).

**The wall is SIX IDENTICAL TILES, three across and two deep, and everything in them is centred** (August 2026, Massimo's call). Every tile is `lg:col-span-4`, the grid takes `sm:auto-rows-fr` so the two rows are the same height as well as the same width, and every widget carries `items-center text-center` at every width — the wall used to be three tile widths (5 / 4 / 3) with left-aligned contents on the desktop and centring only below `sm`, and once the blocks matched, a label at the left edge of one of six identical squares read as the odd one out. Reading order is **Activity · Time saved · Money saved** over **Streak · Academy · Announcements**: the heatmap opens the wall because it is the one tile that answers "have I been working" at a glance, and the two figures read left to right from it.

The tiles: the 13-week **activity heatmap** (`ActivityHeatmap`); **time saved** (per-kind minutes + a task-switching constant, both in `utils/usage.ts`) with a 14-day sparkline (`dailyMinutesSaved`); **money saved** vs the higher of official-API and creator-platform pricing, with a paid-vs-elsewhere bar; a **streak ring** (`StreakRing`, the signature: the arc is the current streak against the member's own best, falling back to a 7-day target before there is a record, with a lit marker riding its head); the **Announcements** log (`AnnouncementsTile`; see the Announcements bullet in the root file); and the **Academy** link.

**A figure tile stacks from its label DOWN, and only its chart takes `mt-auto`.** Bottom-aligning the whole block landed the hero at a different height in each tile, because Money saved's floor art (a bar plus two captions) is taller than a sparkline and pushed its block further up — so the two heroes, captions and deltas sat a good 10px out of line with each other. Text from the top, chart on the floor, and the pair line up by construction. The order inside is **figure → caption → delta pill**: `WidgetDelta` is its own line under the caption rather than a chip on the hero's baseline, which only worked while a tile was wide enough for both (in a four-column tile it wrapped under a 56px hero and read as that number's caption, displacing the real one). **`WidgetLabel` carries no trailing note** — Money saved's credit count was the only one, and `ml-auto` on a note is exactly what a centred label can't have.

**The wall is two rows and must stay two rows** — the whole desktop has to sit inside one window alongside the dock, and a member who has to scroll the landing page to see the heatmap reads it as a page, not a desktop.

**Below `lg` it is a two-across bento** (August 2026): every widget was `col-span-12` there, so a phone got six full-width slabs stacked one under another and the desktop became a page you scroll. Three rows of two, in DOM order. **Nothing in a half-width tile is allowed to wrap, and every second line is the first thing to go** — the phone wall has to fit one screen alongside the dock, and each extra line costs it ~26px. What that means per widget: `WidgetFigure`'s hero drops to 34px below `sm` (48px is wider than the box), **`WidgetDelta` hides entirely**, Activity's generations/active-days tally hides, `SpendBar`'s two captions go to 10px on one `whitespace-nowrap` line, and the Academy card's out-of-flow ↗ goes, since out of flow it reserved no space and simply landed on the last letter of the title. Money saved's sub-line is **one string plus a `hidden sm:inline` tail** rather than two strings: a phone reads "vs official APIs", a desktop gets the rest. Time saved's is a bare `N workdays` at every width — "≈ 7.6 workdays of production and tool-hopping" was a sentence explaining a number that needs no explaining, and the ≈ hedged a figure the hero above it states exactly.

**`ActivityHeatmap` is 13 columns, has no month labels, and SIZES ITSELF to whatever tile it is dropped in.** Every column takes `flex-1 min-w-0 max-w-[18px]` and every cell `aspect-square w-full`, so the blocks come out as big as the row allows and the picture ends where the tile does. Three things were fixed at once here in August 2026 (all Massimo's calls). It ran **26** columns, half a year, which at the tile's own width drew cells too small to read and spent most of the picture on months a member has stopped thinking about — a quarter is the recent run, which is what this widget is looked at for. It drew at a **fixed 11px** per cell above `sm`, making it a fixed-size object in a fluid box: a dead margin down its right wherever the tile was wider (reported as "a massive gap" the moment the legend that had been filling it came out) and an `overflow-x-auto` scrollbar wherever it was narrower — both the same bug, and filling has no width to be right or wrong about. The `max-w` is the one limit, because 13 square cells with no ceiling reach ~24px in a full-width tile and the grid's HEIGHT follows its cell size, which would grow the whole second row with the window; capped, a wide tile centres the grid instead — even margins, which read as placement where a one-sided margin reads as a gap. And the **month labels** went with the 26 columns: at thirteen the picture is "the recent run", not a calendar to find a date in. **There is no legend either.** A `HeatmapLegend` ("Less ▪▪▪▪ More") sat beside the grid at `xl` and is deleted: darker means more is the one thing about this picture nobody has to be told. All of it is CSS-only, so there is no media query in JS.

**Announcements is a LOG, not a door** (August 2026, Massimo's call). It shows the newest `LOG_ROWS` titles with their dates, unread ones dotted and brighter, and opens the panel on click; the list reads **left-aligned inside a centred tile**, because everything else on this wall is a figure or a picture and a stack of ragged titles is the one thing here that is read rather than looked at. It was a disc over the word "Announcements" over "Nothing new", which said nothing about the thing it opened — and once every tile became the same size, it was the one spending a whole square on its own name. It takes its rows off the store's own sorted `items`, so the tile and the panel can never disagree, and the unread dot is **out of flow** at the tile's corner for the same reason `WidgetLabel` has no note. **A figure tile with no floor art yet CENTRES its number** (September 2026, Massimo's call): both charts bail before there is data — `Sparkline` on a flat zero peak, `SpendBar` on nothing spent elsewhere — which left "0 min" pinned under its label with the bottom half of the tile visibly empty, reading as a widget missing a piece rather than one waiting for its first generation. `hasSpark` / `hasSpend` mirror each chart's own bail condition so the tile and its floor can't disagree. The "NOT `mt-auto`" rule above still holds for the populated state, and the pair still line up with each other here — neither has a chart to be pushed up by, which is the exact condition that rule is about. **Academy keeps the centred disc-over-title card** — it is the wall's one pure link and the one tile with nothing of the member's own in it, so it takes no `WidgetLabel` header. **Its disc and title step up from `sm`** (52px / 18px, September 2026, Massimo's call): it carries two short words where the other five carry a figure, so at the five's supporting sizes it read as the quietest thing on a wall of equal squares. The phone keeps the smaller pair — the tile is half a screen wide there and "AI UGC Academy" is one line by a hair. The two wore one shared shape (`SHORTCUT_TILE` in `Widget.tsx`) for exactly as long as they were the same card; when Announcements became a log the constant went back inline, since a shared abstraction with one consumer is worse than a className.

**Centring is now the whole wall's rule, not a phone treatment** — see the six-identical-tiles note above. Two things stay put: **`SpendBar`'s two figures keep `justify-between` at every width**, because they label the two ENDS of the bar above them and centring them detaches each number from what it measures; and **`WidgetLabel`'s icon is monochrome and 13px**, not 15px in the dashboard green, since an eyebrow's whole job is to be quiet and a 15px accent glyph against 11px caps was both the loudest thing on the row and visibly taller than the letters it labels.

**The greeting is ONE face on one line** — `DISPLAY_FONT`, **italic, `font-normal`, `tracking-tighter`** (August 2026, Massimo's call). It was two for a stint, Geist for the salutation and Instrument Serif for the name alone on the reasoning that the name is the one word on the page about this member; the masthead reads better as a single mark, and it went back. It stays at `font-normal`: Instrument Serif ships a single weight, so `font-bold` here only asks the browser to synthesize one, which thickens the strokes without the face ever drawing a real bold — tried for an hour and reverted. Centred on a phone, left-aligned from `sm`, like the wall under it. It's Title Case — "Good Morning", not "Good morning": this is the page's masthead, not a sentence, and it reads as one beside the member's own name in the display face. The line under it is **the date alone** for a member with any activity; "Here's what UGC OS has saved you so far" said nothing the four widgets underneath don't say themselves. The empty-state half stays, since a member with no numbers yet needs telling where they'd come from. It renders 30px on a phone rather than 36, so "Good afternoon, <name>" can't gain a second line and push the bento's last row under the fold. **`WidgetLabel` takes the app's field-label treatment**, not one of its own: 11px, `font-medium`, `text-ink-300` — the same eyebrow `SectionLabel` gives Characters' GENDER / AGE RANGE and every Bank form. It was semibold and a step dimmer, which is one house style too many for a word doing the same job in both places. **Tracking is the one thing it does differently**: `0.07em` against `SectionLabel`'s `widest` (Massimo's call, August 2026). A field label sits over a form and has a column to be spaced across; these sit at the top of a half-width bento tile beside a 13px glyph, where `0.1em` pushed TIME SAVED / ACTIVITY out to the tile's edges and read as stretched rather than as quiet. That budget is what retired the separate **Best streak** and **Active days** tiles: three streak-shaped numbers in a row, now sub-lines on the widgets that own them (the record under the ring it is measured against, the day count in the heatmap's caption). **The crew used to orbit the wall as a solar system** — nine planets on nine rotating arms around a gold sun, hidden below `xl`, one `ORBITS` entry zipped against each `utils/team.ts` member. It is deleted (September 2026, Massimo's call). It was the page's one big swing and it was also permanent motion on the app's default landing page, under a wall of `backdrop-filter` widgets — the one shape [docs/performance.md](../../../docs/performance.md) tells you not to build; measured at ~4 points of one CPU core for as long as the Dashboard was on screen. The dock is the launcher, and it always was below `xl`. Until a kie key is saved, a notification-style connect banner (`ConnectKeyCard` → `ApiKeyGuide`) sits above the widgets. Everything derives from the `usageDays` ledger (see Banks) — the app never writes data. The menu bar shows a flame "N day streak" chip (opens Dashboard) whenever the streak is live.
