# Dashboard — `dashboard/`

System section of UGC OS. This file loads when Claude works with files under `src/apps/dashboard/`; the app-wide rules stay in the root [CLAUDE.md](../../../CLAUDE.md).

## Job

Default landing page, staged as a **desktop in space**: a wallpaper layer (`components/DesktopWallpaper` — three colour blooms, two drifting star layers and a meteor, all dark-mode only except the blooms; classes in `index.css` under `.desktop-*`; shared with Edit, which is why it lives in `components/`, and it takes **no props** — a `tint` prop for a second bloom palette lasted one commit and came out when both pages wanted the same corner. The top-left bloom is **neutral light**, not an accent, so neither page leads on one app's colour: white in dark mode, a soft shadow in light, where a white bloom on a white canvas would be invisible. B-Roll violet and the crab's gold hold the other two corners), the value widgets laid across it, and the crew orbiting as a **solar system** on the right. **Widgets** (`Widget.tsx` — `WIDGET_SHELL` is the one glass material: translucent fill + backdrop-blur/saturate + a 1px inner top highlight, white fill in light mode; `.widget-rise` staggers them in on open): time saved (per-kind minutes + a task-switching constant, both in `utils/usage.ts`) with a 14-day sparkline (`dailyMinutesSaved`), money saved vs the higher of official-API and creator-platform pricing with a paid-vs-elsewhere bar — both heroes carry a green rolling-7-day "+N this week" delta — a **streak ring** (`StreakRing`, the signature: the arc is the current streak against the member's own best, falling back to a 7-day target before there is a record, with a lit marker riding its head), the 26-week activity heatmap, and — stacked in the row's last three columns — the **Announcements** tile (`AnnouncementsTile`, siren glyph + red unread dot, opens the log panel; see the Announcements bullet in the root file) above Academy. Those two are **stacked, not side by side**: the slot is ~177px, which halves into squares too narrow for either title, and giving them a column each drops Activity below nine columns, where the heatmap grows an internal scrollbar. Both are compact icon-left cards with truncating titles, since each gets half the row's height. **The wall is two rows and must stay two rows** — the whole desktop has to sit inside one window alongside the dock, and a member who has to scroll the landing page to see the heatmap reads it as a page, not a desktop. **Below `lg` it is a two-across bento instead** (August 2026): every widget was `col-span-12` there, so a phone got six full-width slabs stacked one under another and the desktop became a page you scroll. Three rows of two, in DOM order — the two figures, then Streak and Activity, then the Announcements/Academy pair unstacked so each gets a square of its own. The whole desktop fits one phone screen again. **Nothing in a half-width tile is allowed to wrap**, and what that costs is spelled out per widget: `WidgetFigure`'s hero drops to 34px below `sm` (48px is wider than the box), `WidgetLabel`'s right-hand note hides, `SpendBar`'s two captions go to 10px on one `whitespace-nowrap` line, Activity's generations/active-days note hides once there IS activity (the empty-state hint stays at every width — an empty grid with no caption reads as broken), and the Academy card's out-of-flow ↗ goes, since out of flow it reserved no space and simply landed on the last letter of the title. The two long sub-lines are **one string plus a `hidden sm:inline` tail** rather than two strings: a phone reads "≈ 7.1 workdays" and "vs official APIs", a desktop gets the rest of the sentence. Activity earns its half tile in `ActivityHeatmap` — below `sm` the cells go to 8px and only the last `PHONE_WEEKS` columns render (the rest take `max-sm:hidden`), which is a quarter of the history at a size you can read rather than half a year squeezed into 128px. All of it is CSS-only, so there is no media query in JS and the desktop wall is untouched by construction.

**The Announcements and Academy cards are SQUARES below `sm`** — `aspect-square`, icon over title over sub-line, centred — and the icon-left row they wear from `sm` up is the same card at a width where a row fits. As 90px strips under two 205px tiles they read as offcuts rather than as the bento's last row. Their titles drop `truncate` there too (`sm:truncate`): the square has the height for a second line and none of the width to lose to an ellipsis. **`DesktopIcons`' column count DIVIDES the nine apps rather than merely fitting them** — three across on a phone (a 3×3 block), nine across from `sm` (one row, 66px+ per 56px tile). Four across left 4/4/1 with one crab stranded on a line of its own, and the old `sm:grid-cols-8` did the same with 8/1. **A tenth app means revisiting both numbers.**

**Everything is CENTRED below `sm`, and nothing is above it** (August 2026, Massimo's call): on a phone the greeting, the date and every stat widget's contents — label, hero figure, sub-line, heatmap — sit on the tile's centre line, because a half-width bento tile reads better that way; from `sm` the wall is the desktop wall and every one of those is left-aligned exactly as it was. It's all `max-sm:` for that reason, so the desktop is untouched by construction. `max-sm:items-center` on a `Widget` makes every child shrink to its content, which is why anything that must span the tile (the figure block, the heatmap row, the spend bar) also says `w-full`. **`SpendBar`'s two figures keep `justify-between` at every width** — they label the two ENDS of the bar above them, and centring them detaches each number from what it measures. **`WidgetLabel`'s icon is monochrome and 13px**, not 15px in the dashboard green: an eyebrow's whole job is to be quiet, and a 15px accent glyph against 11px caps was both the loudest thing on the row and visibly taller than the letters it labels.

**The greeting is two faces on one line** (August 2026): the salutation is chrome and takes the app's own Geist, the NAME takes `DISPLAY_FONT` italic. Centred on a phone, left-aligned from `sm`, like the wall under it. Both were Instrument Serif, which made the whole line ornament and left nothing for the one word on the page that's actually about this member to be set apart by. It's Title Case — "Good Morning", not "Good morning": this is the page's masthead, not a sentence, and it reads as one beside the member's own name in the display face. The line under it is **the date alone** for a member with any activity; "Here's what UGC OS has saved you so far" said nothing the four widgets underneath don't say themselves. The empty-state half stays, since a member with no numbers yet needs telling where they'd come from. It renders 30px on a phone rather than 36 — Geist is a wider face than the serif it replaced, so "Good afternoon, <name>" gained a second line at the old size and pushed the bento's last row under the fold. **`WidgetLabel` takes the app's field-label treatment**, not one of its own: 11px, `font-medium`, `tracking-widest`, `text-ink-300` — the same eyebrow `SectionLabel` gives Characters' GENDER / AGE RANGE and every Bank form. It was semibold, tighter-tracked and a step dimmer, which is one house style too many for a word doing the same job in both places. That budget is what retired the separate **Best streak** and **Active days** tiles: three streak-shaped numbers in a row, now sub-lines on the widgets that own them (the record under the ring it is measured against, the day count in the heatmap's caption). **`SolarSystem.tsx`** is the launcher and the one big swing: eight apps as eight planets on faint orbit rings around a gold sun, each planet the app's dock glyph on a sphere mixed from its own accent. Orbits run 5–15 minutes (halving that read as drift, not depth), the arm and the planet counter-rotate on the same period so glyphs stay upright, and hovering anywhere freezes the system so picking one is never a moving-target problem. **Every orbit arm is `pointer-events-none`** and only the planet takes events back: the arms are eight full-size boxes stacked on each other, so without that the topmost one (Playground, the outermost planet) swallows every hover and click meant for the seven underneath — the whole orrery reads as broken except its last planet. Eight labels in orbit would collide, so one caption slot under it names whatever the cursor is on, crab and all. Geometry is in px, so **below `xl` it's hidden** and `DesktopIcons` renders the plain crew grid instead. Until a kie key is saved, a notification-style connect banner (`ConnectKeyCard` → `ApiKeyGuide`) sits above the widgets. Everything derives from the `usageDays` ledger (see Banks) — the app never writes data. The menu bar shows a flame "N day streak" chip (opens Dashboard) whenever the streak is live.
