# Voiceovers — `voice-studio/`

Create section of UGC OS. This file loads when Claude works with files under `src/apps/voice-studio/`; the app-wide rules stay in the root [CLAUDE.md](../../../CLAUDE.md).

## Job

Script → audio (Gemini TTS). 30 named Google voices, picker **grouped and filtered by gender** (Female 14 / Male 16, split per Google's docs; each voice keeps a vibe descriptor tag). It grouped and filtered by Google's four published **pitch** bands until September 2026 (Massimo's call): pitch is real data and stays on `VoiceOption`, but it isn't the question anyone opens the list with — a UGC ad is cast as a woman or a man reading to camera, and four bands split each of those across four headings, so "a warm female read" meant scanning all four. `PITCH_ORDER` / `PITCH_LABELS` went with the change. The picker generates a short sample **on demand** and caches it per session (`services/previewVoice.ts`) since Gemini has no hosted preview files. **The left column is settings and nothing else, and Generate is at its foot** (September 2026, Massimo's call). It carried a Settings / History `SegmentedToggle` across the top until then; History moved to the output pane (below), which freed the room, and this app stopped being the one whose Generate wasn't at the bottom of its own input column. It is **460px** wide, up from 400 — the last 20px are load-bearing rather than taste, since the model row shares its line with the batch stepper and at 440 the name it exists to show clipped to "Gemini 2.5 Pro T…" at every desktop width (this column is a constant, so that clip never went away). Anything else reading that constant — `BottomPlayer`'s ±10s gating does — has to move with it. It carries **no header band**: see the exception noted against the panel-header rule in the root [CLAUDE.md](../../../CLAUDE.md). Column order: TWO `SectionCard`s (since August 2026): a **Voice** card (the voice row, under a header whose TITLE is the presets opener) and a **Delivery** card (Style → Pace+Accent → Expressiveness) carrying **Reset** on its header, where it sits with the controls it restores rather than a long scroll below them. Tone/context and Scene stay BARE under the cards: they're the two extras at the end, and leaving them uncarded is what says so. They also carry the panel's only `StatusDot`s — every control inside the cards always holds a value, and a permanent row of green dots is decoration. Field labels inside a card are Influencers' small-caps register (`SETTING_LABEL`, mirrored in `Slider`'s own label — keep the two in step), since a 13px sentence-case label under a 13px card title reads as two competing headings. The **Presets pill** loads a saved `voices` bank entry through `PresetPickerView` (`settingsFromPreset` flattens it through `sanitizeVoiceSettings`, so a preset written against an older option list still loads) — voice, delivery params AND its scene/tone in one tap. **Both pickers are centred modals** (`PickerModal`, September 2026, Massimo's call — "can it pop up like that as well"): they were in-panel views that took over this column, which worked while the column held nothing but settings and stopped working the moment it held Generate, since a takeover covers the button. `PickerModal` is `PresetPickerModal`'s geometry with one difference — these hold a LIST of rows rather than a wall of pictures, so the panel is `max-w-xl` and sizes to its content under a `max-h-[86vh]` ceiling; a fixed `h-[86vh]` opens a near-empty box on a bank holding three presets. It is `z-[60]` for the reason spelled out in `PresetPickerModal`: menus portal at that tier, and a panel above it paints over its own. Neither is a `BankPicker` — picking a preset and picking a voice are the same gesture one after the other, so `VoicePickerView` and `PresetPickerView` are the two BODIES of one shell, sharing the search / row shape and the tap-the-avatar sample preview (`useVoicePreview`); presets carry `scene`/`sampleContext`, editable in the Bank's `VoiceForm`. **The card's TITLE is the way in** (September 2026, Massimo's call) — `SectionPresetPill` as `SectionCard`'s `titleNode`, `tone='neutral' size='sm'`, exactly what Playground's Voice card and every section title in Influencers already wear, so a dashed ring plus a chevron is what a list of saved presets looks like app-wide. It replaces a separate **Presets** pill in the left gutter (August 2026), which made a one-control card carry two controls in its header and left the title — the thing the pill fills — as the only part of the row you couldn't click. A preset isn't a setting of its own, it's a shortcut that writes every setting in the panel, which is why it belongs on the group's heading rather than reading as a picker row equal in weight to the one that picks the voice; that leaves the Voice card holding a single control, the one place the app knowingly breaks "don't card a lone control". What survives in the left gutter is `PresetStamp` — the LOADED state only: which preset the settings came from, plus the X that detaches it, never a way in. Its label is capped at 110px and truncates, since a long bank name would otherwise squeeze the title beside it (Delivery's Reset is a header action of the other kind and keeps the right). The stamp (`VoiceSettings.presetId`/`presetLabel`, label-only, never sent to kie) is dropped by ANY hand edit — every control in `SettingsView` goes through one `update()` helper, plus the voice picker, "Reset values" and a history restore — so the row can't name a preset the settings no longer hold. `style` / `pace` / `accent` are custom portaled `Dropdown`s (via `AnchoredPopover`) whose options are kie.ai's **exact** enums — Style (Vocal Smile/Newscaster/Whisper/Empathetic/Promo·Hype/Deadpan, **default Empathetic** — not kie's flat Deadpan, and not Vocal Smile, which held the slot before it), Pace (Natural/Rapid Fire/The Drift/Staccato, **default Rapid Fire**), Accent (Neutral/American Gen·Valley·South/British RP·Brixton/Transatlantic/Australian) — verified at kie.ai/gemini-3.1-flash-tts; don't invent values. `temperature` is the "Expressiveness" slider; `scene`/`sampleContext` optional — but **Tone / Context ships pre-filled** (`DEFAULT_SAMPLE_CONTEXT`), because left blank the model picks its own register. Three defaults changed in August 2026 — style, pace and this — and none of them reaches an existing browser on its own — the panel's settings are a persisted blob, so `DEFAULT_VOICE_SETTINGS` only applies to a slot never written, which after one visit to the tab is nobody. Migration `2026-08-voice-studio-delivery-defaults` (in `settingsStore`) walks the `:voice-studio:settings` keys and moves anyone still on the OLD values, by value, skipping any blob carrying a `presetId` (its pace and tone came from a saved row on purpose). It can NOT live in `sanitizeVoiceSettings`: coercing an empty tone box to the default on every read would make the field impossible to clear.

**The model row** (August 2026) is a shared `ModelPicker row` — the 58px picker-row geometry — and since September 2026 (Massimo's call) it sits at the FOOT of the settings column, in `GenerateBar`, sharing one line with the batch stepper directly above the Generate button. It led the column for a month, on the reasoning that the voice, the delivery and the direction are all settings OF the model; it reads better above the button that spends it, beside the other property of THIS press. It takes `flex-1` with the stepper at `size='xl'` (the same 58px), so the pair is one line by construction rather than by a copied number — the shape Scripts already uses above its own Generate. Uncarded (a border around one control says nothing) and unlabelled: a picker row already shows the model's name over its hint, so a small-caps "MODEL" said the word twice, and there is no `StatusDot` for the same reason — there is always a resolved model and a permanent green dot is decoration. Two entries — **Gemini 2.5 Pro TTS** (default) and **Gemini 3.1 Flash TTS** — and `task === 'tts'` joins image in `ModelPicker`'s `flatList`, or a two-model list would print the starred one twice over a hairline. They take the **identical** request body off the identical voice/style/pace/accent catalogs, so `buildVoiceInput` never branches on the model; they also share one rate card, so nothing about the choice is a cost decision — which is why the rows print **no credit estimate** (`task === 'tts'` sets `ModelPicker`'s `hideCredits`). TTS is metered on the script you actually send, so a row could only ever quote a nominal 1000 characters: a number that is neither what this run costs nor different between the two entries. The Generate button prices the real script. `startVoiceTask` resolves the pick through `resolveTtsModel()` and **snapshots it on the in-flight entry** (and on the history row's `modelId`), so a task that outlives a model swap — or a refresh — still finishes and is priced as the model that actually ran it; an entry or row with no `modelId` predates the picker and defaults to Flash. The character count (above the script box since August 2026) used to carry the model name as fine print under it and no longer does: the picker names it, and one pane saying it twice is one too many. `voiceId` holds the Gemini `voice_name`. **`seedColor` is the one avatar fill**, shared by both pickers, the settings row, History's cards, the details view and the player's play button — a deterministic three-stop METAL ramp under a soft top-left bloom (the same diffuse highlight `.glass-fill` lays, never a tight specular, which reads as plastic gloss). It was a rainbow — two hues out of ten spanning the whole wheel, so thirty voices were thirty saturated discs in every colour at once, the loudest thing in a panel of grey chrome — and became metal in September 2026 (Massimo's call). **The metal is picked by GENDER, and that is the point**: the picker groups and filters by Female / Male, so the disc has to agree with the heading above it. Women take the WARM metals (rose gold, copper, champagne, antique gold, blush bronze), men the COOL ones (silver, steel, gunmetal, graphite, onyx) — one material family at one weight, a warm/cool split and never a pink/blue one, which would drop a stock gender cliché into an app that has none anywhere else. Within a gender the metal is seeded off the id, so a voice keeps its own disc. No stop is paler than ~#DDE2E8: these sit on a white card in light mode, and a near-white disc disappears into it. Anything with no gender on file falls to the cool set. Play/pause glyphs are **filled** (`fill-current`) on every surface, with the triangle nudged `translate-x-px` — its visual centre of mass sits left of its bounding box, so a centred play button looks off-centre without it. `sanitizeVoiceSettings` coerces persisted style/pace/accent that predate an option-list change. **History is a card list, not thin rows** — a voiceover has no thumbnail, so the card carries the clip itself: a 48px play button (the voice's `seedColor` avatar doubles as it, so the colour still says who is speaking) over the voice name + "3h ago · 0:06", and the script preview under it. **The waveform belongs to playback** — the strip is collapsed until the play button is pressed and closes again when the clip ends, because a card at rest is a thing you read, not a thing you watch. `components/Waveform.tsx` (shared with Playground's music tiles since September 2026 — `playedClass` picks the accent) draws the **whole clip at rest and fills it left to right as it plays** (SoundCloud-style), and a click anywhere on it seeks. The peaks are decoded once per asset from the audio itself (`waveformPeaks` in `utils/audioPlayback.ts`: fetch → `decodeAudioData` on an **Offline**AudioContext → 56 peak buckets, square-root curved so it isn't a flat line with two spikes, normalised to the loudest bar, cached by asset ref). It replaced a live spectrum analyzer that failed in both directions: the analyzer's default ceiling (`maxDecibels` −30) sits BELOW where a normalised voiceover plays, so every band pinned at 255 and a loud clip rendered as 44 bars flat out at full height — and routing the `<audio>` element through an `AnalyserNode` meant a suspended AudioContext played the clip **silently**. Decoded peaks touch nothing on the playback path, so that whole class of bug is gone with it. `utils/audioPlayback.ts` holds the plumbing (it lived here as `audio.ts` until Playground's music history wanted the same player): the one-clip-at-a-time slot (the audio counterpart of `useInlineVideo`'s rule — cards and `BottomPlayer` register a pause fn, so play on one stops the other), `resolveAudioUrl`, and the peak decoder. **Never trade sound for a visual** is the rule that shaped it: nothing the waveform does may touch the element that's playing.

**One press can fire up to three voiceovers** (`BatchCountStepper`, `max={3}`). It sits in `GenerateBar` at the foot of the settings column, sharing the model row's line at `size='xl'` — a property of THIS press, like the button's own cost pill, not a saved delivery setting, which is why it sits beside the model rather than among the delivery controls. It carries **no label** (September 2026): stacked with the word "Voiceovers" it measured 151px and left the model row 240px for a name wanting 112, so the row whose entire job is naming the model clipped to "Gemini 2…". The button directly under it says the noun ("Generate 3 Voiceovers") and `noun` still spells it out in the tooltip, so only the width was lost. Same voice, same params, same words, and the delivery still lands differently every time, so choosing between three is the job; three was already what members did by pressing Generate three times. `handleGenerate` calls `runOneVoice` N times and each read is its own kie task, its own in-flight entry and its own history row — History's In-progress section reports the batch with nothing new built.

**Generate has the bottom row to itself**, full width, and the progress bar and the error sit directly above it — they report on that press and belong beside it. It kept its noun at every width once it left the editor column: there it shared a 375px phone line with the stepper and the player, which is what clipped it to "Generat…". **`handleGenerate` flips the output pane to History** (and, on a phone, to the output pane at all) — Generate now lives in the settings pane, which on a phone is the ONLY pane on screen, so without the flip a press looks like nothing happened; History is where the queue reports itself. The character count stays above the script box it counts, in the editor's action row opposite New and Enhance. The word "characters" is still desktop-only.

**The player is the OUTPUT column's footer** (September 2026), a full-width band under both tabs — it is the transport for whatever is playing, whichever list is on screen. It rode inside the generate row as that row's third item until Generate moved to the settings column, and before that it was a full-width band under that row. What it gave up when it moved into the row, and has not taken back, is the **avatar + script-preview block** on its left: the play button is filled with the voice's own `seedColor` instead, the same trick the History cards use, so the control still says who is speaking, and the details button (`≡`) is where the words are. The **±10s pair renders from `lg` up** — a viewport breakpoint is an exact proxy here, since the settings column is a fixed 460px and this bar is therefore the viewport minus a constant. The threshold was `2xl` while the bar shared its row with a Generate button and had ~160px for the scrubber; with the row to itself it has ~544px at 1024px, so the pair earns its place much earlier. The phone drops the details button too. The scrubber keeps `mx-1` because its thumb is `-translate-x-1/2` and `touch:opacity-100` — half of it hangs outside the track at 0:00, and on a phone that put it on top of the elapsed-time label. It **drags as well as clicks** (August 2026): one `seekToClientX` serves the press and every move, and the press takes **pointer capture**, without which the drag dies the instant the pointer leaves a ~150px sliver wedged between the elapsed label and three icon buttons. Its grab area is the `py-2` around the 6px bar, held off the row's height by `-my-2` — a target the height of the line itself is a click you have to aim. The played portion and the thumb are **`bg-voice-500`**, the literal app accent, not the `ink` ramp: `ink-100` is near-black in light mode, so the bar read as dead chrome beside the play button it belongs to, and 500 is the one rung that doesn't flip between themes.

**The right column is the output pane: Script or History behind one `SegmentedToggle`** (September 2026, Massimo's call). History was a tab of the settings column, stacked behind the controls that produce it; it belongs beside the other output. `HistoryDetailsView` rides on top of that body, opaque, the same shape the settings column used when it owned History, and opening one (from a card or from the player) puts that pane on screen. `EditorArea` is now purely the script — the picker row, the OR divider, the character-count / New / Enhance action row and the textarea, with no footer of its own.
