# Voiceovers — `voice-studio/`

Create section of UGC OS. This file loads when Claude works with files under `src/apps/voice-studio/`; the app-wide rules stay in the root [CLAUDE.md](../../../CLAUDE.md).

## Job

Script → audio (Gemini TTS). 30 named Google voices, picker **grouped by gender** (Female 14 / Male 16, split per Google's docs; each voice keeps a vibe descriptor tag). The picker generates a short sample **on demand** and caches it per session (`services/previewVoice.ts`) since Gemini has no hosted preview files. Side panel order (it sits on the LEFT, with the script editor to its right — the only app whose controls column isn't the input column): a bare **Model** row at the very top, then TWO `SectionCard`s (since August 2026): a **Voice** card (the voice row, with **Presets** as a pill on the card's header) and a **Delivery** card (Style → Pace+Accent → Expressiveness) carrying **Reset** on its header, where it sits with the controls it restores rather than a long scroll below them. Tone/context and Scene stay BARE under the cards: they're the two extras at the end, and leaving them uncarded is what says so. They also carry the panel's only `StatusDot`s — every control inside the cards always holds a value, and a permanent row of green dots is decoration. Field labels inside a card are Influencers' small-caps register (`SETTING_LABEL`, mirrored in `Slider`'s own label — keep the two in step), since a 13px sentence-case label under a 13px card title reads as two competing headings. The **Presets pill** loads a saved `voices` bank entry through `PresetPickerView` (`settingsFromPreset` flattens it through `sanitizeVoiceSettings`, so a preset written against an older option list still loads) — voice, delivery params AND its scene/tone in one tap. It is **not** a `BankPicker` slide-over: picking a preset and picking a voice are the same gesture one after the other, so both are in-panel views that take over the side panel (`SidePanel` owns both flags and hides the tab strip while either is up), sharing the header / search / row shape and the tap-the-avatar sample preview (`useVoicePreview`); presets carry `scene`/`sampleContext`, editable in the Bank's `VoiceForm`. It is **Influencers' `PresetPillButton` shape on the `SectionCard` header**, not a second full-width row under the voice (August 2026): a preset isn't a setting of its own, it's a shortcut that writes every setting in the panel, so it belongs on the group's header rather than reading as a picker row equal in weight to the one that picks the voice. That leaves the Voice card holding a single control — the one place the app knowingly breaks "don't card a lone control", because the card header IS the pill's home. Its label is capped at 110px and truncates: it sits in the **left** gutter of the header's `grid-cols-[1fr_auto_1fr]` (the shortcut you take *before* picking a voice by hand, so it reads ahead of the title; Delivery's Reset is a header action of the other kind and keeps the right), and a long bank name would otherwise squeeze the title beside it. The pill's stamp (`VoiceSettings.presetId`/`presetLabel`, label-only, never sent to kie) is dropped by ANY hand edit — every control in `SettingsView` goes through one `update()` helper, plus the voice picker, "Reset values" and a history restore — so the row can't name a preset the settings no longer hold. `style` / `pace` / `accent` are custom portaled `Dropdown`s (via `AnchoredPopover`) whose options are kie.ai's **exact** enums — Style (Vocal Smile/Newscaster/Whisper/Empathetic/Promo·Hype/Deadpan, **default Empathetic** — not kie's flat Deadpan, and not Vocal Smile, which held the slot before it), Pace (Natural/Rapid Fire/The Drift/Staccato, **default Rapid Fire**), Accent (Neutral/American Gen·Valley·South/British RP·Brixton/Transatlantic/Australian) — verified at kie.ai/gemini-3.1-flash-tts; don't invent values. `temperature` is the "Expressiveness" slider; `scene`/`sampleContext` optional — but **Tone / Context ships pre-filled** (`DEFAULT_SAMPLE_CONTEXT`), because left blank the model picks its own register. Three defaults changed in August 2026 — style, pace and this — and none of them reaches an existing browser on its own — the panel's settings are a persisted blob, so `DEFAULT_VOICE_SETTINGS` only applies to a slot never written, which after one visit to the tab is nobody. Migration `2026-08-voice-studio-delivery-defaults` (in `settingsStore`) walks the `:voice-studio:settings` keys and moves anyone still on the OLD values, by value, skipping any blob carrying a `presetId` (its pace and tone came from a saved row on purpose). It can NOT live in `sanitizeVoiceSettings`: coercing an empty tone box to the default on every read would make the field impossible to clear.

**The model row** (August 2026) is a shared `ModelPicker row` — the 58px picker-row geometry, so it stacks with the Voice row below it — at the top of the settings column, above the Voice card. It's there rather than inside a card because the border around one control says nothing, and it's *above* the cards because the voice, the delivery and the direction are all settings OF the model it names; it carries the panel's small-caps label and no `StatusDot`, since there is always a resolved model and a permanent green dot is decoration. Two entries — **Gemini 2.5 Pro TTS** (default) and **Gemini 3.1 Flash TTS** — and `task === 'tts'` joins image in `ModelPicker`'s `flatList`, or a two-model list would print the starred one twice over a hairline. They take the **identical** request body off the identical voice/style/pace/accent catalogs, so `buildVoiceInput` never branches on the model; they also share one rate card, so nothing about the choice is a cost decision — which is why the rows print **no credit estimate** (`task === 'tts'` sets `ModelPicker`'s `hideCredits`). TTS is metered on the script you actually send, so a row could only ever quote a nominal 1000 characters: a number that is neither what this run costs nor different between the two entries. The Generate button prices the real script. `startVoiceTask` resolves the pick through `resolveTtsModel()` and **snapshots it on the in-flight entry** (and on the history row's `modelId`), so a task that outlives a model swap — or a refresh — still finishes and is priced as the model that actually ran it; an entry or row with no `modelId` predates the picker and defaults to Flash. The character count (above the script box since August 2026) used to carry the model name as fine print under it and no longer does: the picker names it, and one pane saying it twice is one too many. `voiceId` holds the Gemini `voice_name`. `sanitizeVoiceSettings` coerces persisted style/pace/accent that predate an option-list change. **History is a card list, not thin rows** — a voiceover has no thumbnail, so the card carries the clip itself: a 48px play button (the voice's `seedColor` avatar doubles as it, so the colour still says who is speaking) over the voice name + "3h ago · 0:06", and the script preview under it. **The waveform belongs to playback** — the strip is collapsed until the play button is pressed and closes again when the clip ends, because a card at rest is a thing you read, not a thing you watch. `Waveform.tsx` draws the **whole clip at rest and fills it left to right as it plays** (SoundCloud-style), and a click anywhere on it seeks. The peaks are decoded once per asset from the audio itself (`waveformPeaks` in `audio.ts`: fetch → `decodeAudioData` on an **Offline**AudioContext → 56 peak buckets, square-root curved so it isn't a flat line with two spikes, normalised to the loudest bar, cached by asset ref). It replaced a live spectrum analyser that failed in both directions: the analyser's default ceiling (`maxDecibels` −30) sits BELOW where a normalised voiceover plays, so every band pinned at 255 and a loud clip rendered as 44 bars flat out at full height — and routing the `<audio>` element through an `AnalyserNode` meant a suspended AudioContext played the clip **silently**. Decoded peaks touch nothing on the playback path, so that whole class of bug is gone with it. `audio.ts` holds the plumbing: the one-clip-at-a-time slot (the audio counterpart of `useInlineVideo`'s rule — cards and `BottomPlayer` register a pause fn, so play on one stops the other), `resolveAudioUrl`, and the peak decoder. **Never trade sound for a visual** is the rule that shaped it: nothing the waveform does may touch the element that's playing.

**One press can fire up to three voiceovers** (`BatchCountStepper`, `max={3}`, in the footer rather than in the settings column — it's a property of THIS press, like the button's own cost pill, not a saved delivery setting). It sits **to the LEFT of Generate** and is exactly the button's height, `size='fill'` inside an `items-stretch` pair so the match is by construction rather than by a copied number, with the count `stacked` over the word **Voiceovers** — a count reads as a setting on the way to Generate, so it sits on the approach to it rather than trailing behind, and it names what it counts in the same word the button does. That word is **desktop-only** (`labelClassName="max-md:hidden"`), and so is the button's own noun: a 375px row can't fit a labelled stepper, "Generate 3 Voiceovers" and a credits pill, and what gave way was the button's label, which rendered as "Generat…". On a phone the stepper is a bare −/+ and the button reads "Generate 3" — the number is what the press spends, so it stays; the noun is said by the app, the pane tab and the panel above. Same voice, same params, same words, and the delivery still lands differently every time, so choosing between three is the job; three was already what members did by pressing Generate three times. `handleGenerate` calls `runOneVoice` N times and each read is its own kie task, its own in-flight entry and its own history row — the existing In-progress section above the finished rows reports the batch with nothing new built. Capped at 3 because past that you're auditioning, not choosing. **The footer holds the stepper, Generate and the PLAYER.** The character count sat on its right until August 2026 and now rides the action row **above the script box**, opposite New and Enhance: it belongs to the script (it moves as you type, and that's the panel you type in), and as a third item in the footer it was what squeezed the button's label on a phone. The model name lived in that same right-hand slot as fine print — and, before that, as a bordered pill beside Generate — back when there was one TTS model and the line never changed; the settings column's model row names it now. The word "characters" is still desktop-only.

**The player rides the generate row** (August 2026), as its third item, taking whatever the button leaves (`flex-1` on the player, `md:flex-none` on the button, whose desktop padding came down from `px-16` to `px-10` to fund it). It used to be a full-width band UNDER that row — a whole strip of the editor column spent on a control the row was already wide enough to hold, on a desktop where the button ends barely halfway across. The row is `flex-wrap`, so below `md` — where the stepper and the button already fill the line — the player drops to its own line underneath, which is where it used to live full-time; nothing needed a JS media query. What it gave up to fit is the **avatar + script-preview block** on its left: the play button is filled with the voice's own `seedColor` instead, the same trick the History cards use, so the control still says who is speaking, and the details button (`≡`) is where the words are. The **±10s pair renders from `2xl` up only** — a viewport breakpoint is an exact proxy here, since the side panel is a fixed 400px and this bar is therefore the viewport minus a constant: below that, the two buttons and their gaps are ~80px out of the ~150px the scrubber has, and on a six-second read a clickable scrubber beats a jump longer than the clip. The phone drops the details button too. The scrubber keeps `mx-1` because its thumb is `-translate-x-1/2` and `touch:opacity-100` — half of it hangs outside the track at 0:00, and on a phone that put it on top of the elapsed-time label. It **drags as well as clicks** (August 2026): one `seekToClientX` serves the press and every move, and the press takes **pointer capture**, without which the drag dies the instant the pointer leaves a ~150px sliver wedged between the elapsed label and three icon buttons. Its grab area is the `py-2` around the 6px bar, held off the row's height by `-my-2` — a target the height of the line itself is a click you have to aim. The played portion and the thumb are **`bg-voice-500`**, the literal app accent, not the `ink` ramp: `ink-100` is near-black in light mode, so the bar read as dead chrome beside the blue play button it belongs to, and 500 is the one rung that doesn't flip between themes.
